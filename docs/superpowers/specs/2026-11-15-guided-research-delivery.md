missing. The incumbent physiological scoring pipeline is unchanged. Only a deliberate calibration

new numerical logic; Android capture/research UI and full Android SDK compilation are not delivered.

oracles with Kotlin literal and Swift reverse pins, source hygiene and iPhone/macOS app compilation.

NROOP_GUIDED_STEP_CALIBRATION.md, NROOP_SLEEP_CLOCK_STRESS.md and NROOP_EXERCISE_GPS_RESEARCH.md.
